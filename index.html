Questions

The things people ask us first.

You'll receive a written Affiliated Business Arrangement Disclosure at the time of any referral.

What does escrow actually do?

Escrow is the neutral party that holds the deposit and the documents while both sides complete their obligations. We collect the funds, prepare and coordinate signing, clear title conditions, liens, and payoffs, then record the deed and disburse the money. Nothing moves until the conditions in your contract are satisfied. More detail at hw-escrow.com.

How long does a typical closing take?

It depends on financing and contingencies. A financed purchase commonly runs 30 to 45 days from accepted offer; cash purchases can close faster. Because the loan and escrow run in the same house here, we catch conditions early rather than discovering them the week of closing. Your specific timeline is set by the contract.

What do I need for pre-approval?

Generally recent pay stubs, two years of W-2s or tax returns, recent bank and asset statements, and authorization to pull credit. Self-employed borrowers should expect additional documentation. Rita will send an exact list for your situation. Do not email sensitive documents — she'll provide a secure upload link.

How much do I need for a down payment?

It varies by program. Some loans allow as little as 3 to 5 percent down; others require more, and jumbo loans common in this market typically require larger down payments. Down payments under 20 percent usually mean mortgage insurance. Use the calculator above to model scenarios, then let Rita confirm what you actually qualify for.

What does it cost to work with you?

Brokerage commission is negotiated and set out in your listing or representation agreement. Escrow and loan fees are disclosed in writing before you commit, and lending costs appear on your Loan Estimate. We'll walk you through how the three fit together up front rather than at the signing table.
